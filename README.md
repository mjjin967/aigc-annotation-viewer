Viewing the image annotations

Open a terminal in that folder and run:
Mac/Linux: `python3 -m http.server 8000`
Windows: `py -m http.server 8000`

In your browser, go to `http://localhost:8000`.
Pick your name from the Person dropdown.
Move through your images with the ← → arrow keys or the Previous/Next buttons. To jump to an image, type its number in the box at the top right. The annotations for each image appear in the panel on the right.
